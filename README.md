# Contacts Cleanup

A free Thunderbird add-on that tidies your address book and gets your contacts ready for chat:

- finds duplicate contacts and helps merge them
- puts phone numbers into one format
- fills in missing display names
- adds chat addresses to contact cards: XMPP addresses, and Cheogram addresses made from phone numbers, so you can text phone numbers over XMPP

Nothing changes until you look at a proposed fix and apply it, and every applied change can be undone.

With [OMEMO for Thunderbird Chat](https://github.com/precisioncrab/thunderbird-omemo) installed, it can also name your XMPP chat contacts from your address book, and you can chat with them end-to-end encrypted with OMEMO.

**Status:** planned, not yet built. See [docs/PLAN.md](docs/PLAN.md).

It uses only Thunderbird's official add-on APIs.
